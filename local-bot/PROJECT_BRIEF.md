# Game Content Brief

This document is the source of truth for creating public content about JOOP. Keep all claims factual. Do not invent or assume information that is not included here.

## Project

- **Game name:** JOOP
- **One-line description:** A medieval fantasy puzzle game where the player must plan one sequence of movements that works across multiple different worlds.
- **Genre:** Puzzle / Strategy / Fantasy
- **Platforms:** PC and Web (a browser-playable prototype is available). Other platforms are not confirmed.
- **Audience:** Players who enjoy thoughtful puzzles, strategic planning, fantasy settings, and indie games.
- **Official links:** itch.io game page — URL not provided in this brief. Studio: PlungerHead (@kd3k318dse) on X.

## Public facts

- JOOP is an indie game being developed by PlungerHead.
- The game features a unique multi-world puzzle mechanic: movements made in one world are replayed in other worlds with different layouts and hazards.
- Players control a king who must navigate dangerous worlds and rescue people from a kingdom attacked by a dark sorcerer.
- A playable browser prototype has been published on itch.io.
- The game is still in development, and its visuals are being developed and improved.

## Current status

- **Development stage:** Prototype / Early Development
- **Current focus:** Developing the dungeon environments, gameplay content, and visual assets. Improving the playable demo and expanding the game's content.
- **Release information:** No official release date announced.
